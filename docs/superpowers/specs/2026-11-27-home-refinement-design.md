# Homepage refinement

The user approved progressing the proposed quiet editorial direction: reduce competing information, retain the warm palette, and integrate the decorative wave.

## Approaches
A spacing-only pass leaves competing sections intact. A technology-led redesign changes the site's established character. The selected approach preserves the warm research-notebook identity while simplifying hierarchy.

## Design
Keep one hero action linking to published work. Replace the focus card with a quiet sentence below the introduction. Preserve all published research links and their qualifications. Present supporting tools as border-separated rows, with repository identifiers and categories left on the project page. Show recent writing with one short excerpt and no repeated category tags. Keep full blog listings unchanged.

Wave colors come from theme CSS tokens: grey-brown in light mode and muted copper in dark mode. Reduce mesh density and pulse strength. Preserve pause, reduced motion, offscreen suspension and no-JavaScript fallbacks. Remove the focus-card tile animation; retain the short heading and frame entrance.

English remains the default, with aligned Chinese copy. No dependencies, research data, external links or article bodies change. Verify both locales, both themes, narrow screens, keyboard navigation and animation lifecycle.
