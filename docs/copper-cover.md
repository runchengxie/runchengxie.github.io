# Copper cover

The personal homepage shares warm paper, brown text and coffee/cream dark surfaces with the small-cap research publication. Its left-aligned introduction remains the primary content. The cover spans the browser width and fills the first screen below the measured navigation height; shorter screens can grow to keep content readable. Project and article lists retain the original content width. Copper hairlines and sparse contours occupy the right and lower parts of the cover, with stronger masking behind text.

This adapts the personal site's existing synthetic Canvas 2D wave. It does not import third-party rendering code or market data. The field uses 1,508 points on desktop and 576 on narrow screens, a 1.5 pixel-ratio cap and a 30fps scheduling target with retained remainder. Pause persistence, reduced motion, offscreen/hidden-page suspension and static fallbacks remain in place.

The personal site's article typography remains unchanged. The research site separately adopts its narrower reading column and typography hierarchy; the two repositories have no runtime dependency on each other.

Pointer proximity turns a sparse patch of hairlines into small candle bodies with upper and lower wicks. About one fifth of marks are eligible, with muted brick/sage colour confined to the bodies. A squared distance falloff and eased hover strength let columns grow and recede without adding points or a rendering loop. The candles are decorative, not market data. Wave displacement selects the direction colour; English defaults to green-up and Chinese to red-up. An existing manual choice from the small-cap site on the same origin takes precedence and updates across tabs. The background stays copper. Each mark uses a rectangle instead of an arc, and a candle adds only one rectangle; there is no chart library or additional animation loop. Touch input does not activate them, and pause/reduced-motion settings stop the interaction with the wave.
